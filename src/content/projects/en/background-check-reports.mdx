## The challenge

Before working with a person or a vehicle, the operation needed to know their **background** — records in
official and restrictive lists, and the status of vehicles — and to do it for many people and vehicles at
once, without someone checking each source by hand.

## How it works

1. **Request.** A user asks for a report by **ID document** (people) or **plate** (vehicles), one at a time
   or as a batch.
2. **Report API.** A **Flask** service receives the request and hands it to a **per-country processor**
   (starting with Colombia), so each country's rules and sources stay isolated and new ones can be added.
3. **Launch.** The processor submits the query to the **TusDatos.co** API, which aggregates hundreds of
   official sources. Lookups take time, so the query runs asynchronously instead of making the user wait.
4. **Status checker.** **Airflow** background and batch jobs poll the status of each query until its
   results are ready — large batches never block the platform.
5. **Transformer.** A transformation step normalizes the raw results into a single report structure and
   **enriches them with our own internal data**, giving a more complete picture than the external source
   alone. Results are stored in **MongoDB**.
6. **Report.** A **React** view presents the final background report for the person or vehicle.

## My role

I **led the project and developed it**: designed the integration and the processing flow, defined how jobs
run in batch and in the background, and built the service, the pipelines and the report view.

## Result

**Reliable, enriched background reports** on demand — for a single person or vehicle or for whole batches —
combining official sources with internal data, and generated in the background without slowing down the
platform.
