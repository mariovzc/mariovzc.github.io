## The challenge

**Corus Dental** created _Bright_, a dental veneer system built around a digital workflow. Dentists needed a
way to show patients what their new smile could look like during the consultation — a visual, interactive
proposal instead of an abstract explanation — and Corus needed to control who can use the tool.

## What I did

- **Architecture and project lead.** Defined the architecture of the app and the admin platform, planned
  timelines and milestones, and coordinated the work with the product designer from mockups to release.
- **Native iOS app (iPhone and iPad).** Built with Swift and Objective-C: license-based sign-in, a guided
  photo capture flow (close-ups of the central teeth) and an interactive simulation of the proposed smile,
  which the dentist can send with the simulation and centre details.
- **Admin panel.** A Node.js + Express API with a React back office where Corus manages users and
  licenses — who can access the app and for how long.
- **Store releases.** Builds and App Store submissions for iPhone and iPad.

## Result

A sales and consultation tool that turns a smile-design concept into something the patient can see on the
spot, with access controlled centrally through licenses. The app is published under the Saludtec developer
account.
