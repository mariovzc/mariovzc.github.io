## The challenge

**NUBALAB S.A.S** runs a web platform where dental imaging centers deliver results — panoramic X-rays,
periapicals, 3D STL models — to patients and dentists. Several imaging brands use it, and each one needed
its own app on the App Store and Google Play, without maintaining a separate native codebase per brand.

## What I built

- **One Flutter wrapper, many apps.** A reusable Flutter shell that renders the existing web platform
  inside a native app, configured per brand (name, icon, colors, URL). The same code ships
  **Clin2Cloud**, **Odontólogo Digital** and **Imágenes Dentales**.
- **Web support.** Helped adapt parts of the PHP/jQuery platform so it works well inside the app.
- **Automated releases with Codemagic.** A CI/CD pipeline that builds, signs and uploads the iOS and
  Android binaries, so a new brand or version doesn't mean a manual release.
- **Store management end to end.** Developer accounts, certificates and signing, store listings,
  screenshots, privacy forms, review submissions and follow-up until approval.

## Result

Three apps live on both stores within **two months**, all from a single codebase — new brands can be
added with configuration instead of new development. The apps are published under the Saludtec
developer account.

- [Clin2Cloud on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/co/app/clin2cloud/id6475777516)
- [Odontólogo Digital on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/co/app/odont%C3%B3logo-digital/id6478245021)
